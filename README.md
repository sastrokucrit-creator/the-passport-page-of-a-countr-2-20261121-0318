# The Passport Page of a Country That Isn't There

**Republic of Zafran** — an invented passport spread for Layla Nur Hadri, a saffron merchant of the oasis republic of Zafran.

The spread: a data page (page 2) with portrait, trilingual fields, observations and a machine-readable strip with real ICAO check digits; and a visas page (page 5) with a water-allocation endorsement, a 6 ZFR well-tax stamp, a short-stay visa from the coastal republic of Telassa, and eight entry/exit stamps that tell one coherent travel history — the caravan road to the saffron market of Kharun (Oct 2023) and to Telassa (Nov 2024), home again each time.

Every stamp falls after the passport's issue (08 MAR 2023) and before its expiry (07 MAR 2033); the visa's validity window covers the Telassa trip exactly.

`main.ts` hands in the committed `entry.png` (1024×1024). `page.html` draws the spread; `portrait.jpg` is the holder's photograph. See `NOTES.md` for the top-3 study and the verification record.
